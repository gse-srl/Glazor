sakila.sqlite is the Sakila sample database, the DVD rental store of the MySQL samples, in the SQLite build of
https://github.com/bradleygrant/sakila-sqlite3; the demo reads it only.

The schema and the data are the Sakila sample database, version 0.8, by MySQL AB, now Oracle and/or its affiliates,
under the New BSD license (https://dev.mysql.com/doc/sakila/en/sakila-license.html), whose notice follows as its
sakila-schema.sql states it. The SQLite port, by DB Software Laboratory (http://www.etl-tools.com, "License: BSD"), was
carried on by jOOQ (https://github.com/jOOQ/sakila) and built into this database by Bradley Grant, under the BSD
3-Clause License that follows the first.
